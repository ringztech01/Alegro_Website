Integrated Construction
& Engineering Services

Everything you need to get built under one roof.

From rural roads to dual carriageways, we build flexible, rigid and hybrid pavements with drainage and erosion control. Our own asphalt plant, graders, rollers and tippers keep surfacing and base works on programme.

Bridges and culverts, earthworks, piling and foundations, walkways, reservoirs and dams. We rebuild failed crossings with new decks, guard rails and approaches that restore access for communities.

Design, bills of quantities, construction and supervision of estates, hospitals, malls and halls, from structural carcass through finishing and furnishing.

Geophysical survey, deep borehole drilling, treatment plants, storage tanks and reticulation, using our own drilling rigs and resistivity survey equipment.

Rural electrification, overhead lines, transformers and solar street lighting systems for public clients and communities.

Planning, site supervision, quality control and procurement for public and private clients, so one team answers for schedule, cost and quality.
