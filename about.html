About Alegro Construction

Building Nigeria’s roads, bridges and homes since 2014.

Alegro Construction Limited carries out construction, engineering and supply work for public and private clients, from our head office in New Owerri, Imo State.

Explore services

Quality, timely and excellent service delivery is our pride.

Our motto - Alegro Construction Limited

Quality in, quality out

Alegro was incorporated in Nigeria on 26 June 2014 (RC 1200622) to carry out construction, engineering and supply work, with one accountable team from survey and design through construction, handover and maintenance.

Plant & equipment

Our own fleet on every site

We own our asphalt plant, concrete batching plants, excavators, dozers, graders, rollers and borehole drilling rigs, so programmes don’t wait on hire and our crews know their machines. That control is how we keep roads, drainage and structures on schedule and to specification.

Our vision:
Infrastructure that lasts.

Excellence isn’t achieved overnight. It’s earned by delivering quality work on time, project after project. On that foundation we’re building roads, bridges and buildings that serve communities for decades.

  • 2014

    Alegro is incorporated

    Alegro Construction Limited is registered in Nigeria with the Corporate Affairs Commission and sets up its head office in New Owerri, Imo State.

  • 2022

    Riverine communities connected

    We build a 2 km rigid concrete walkway at Ikorigho, Ilaje LGA, Ondo State, for the Federal Ministry of Niger Delta Affairs.

  • 2023

    Hospital and commercial buildings

    We deliver the NHIS Building at University College Hospital, Ibadan, and carcass works on Plaza A at The Kubwa Mall, Abuja.

  • 2025

    Housing and skills centres

    We build four detached duplexes for NAF Housing & Construction Company and complete a youth centre for NASRDA in Taraba State.

  • 2025

    Solar street lighting

    We install solar street lighting across Owo/Ose Federal Constituency, Ondo State, for the Benin-Owena River Basin Development Authority.
